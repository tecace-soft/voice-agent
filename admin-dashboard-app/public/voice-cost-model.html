Break-even · Self-hosted realtime-mini vs Retell

Rate sheet & method

Every option is normalized to USD per minute of live conversation, assuming a minute splits ~30s caller + 30s agent speech. Realtime audio is billed at OpenAI's stated 600 tok/min (caller) and 1,200 tok/min (agent). Realtime context re-billing grows with call length — each agent turn re-ingests the conversation so far — which is why cost rises super-linearly and caching matters. Cascade & Retell scale roughly linearly.
